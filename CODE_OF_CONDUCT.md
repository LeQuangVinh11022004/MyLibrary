# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Trang Sach to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and respectful.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Explain disagreements constructively and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information.
- Follow the repository's security reporting guidance for vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to address behavior that violates this code. Decisions will be made with the goal of keeping the project safe and constructive.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
